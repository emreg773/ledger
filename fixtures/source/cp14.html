Penalties

Failure-to-pay penalty: $35.40. We charge this penalty when you don't pay the tax shown on your return by the due date. It is generally 0.5% of the unpaid tax for each month or part of a month the tax stays unpaid, up to 25%.

Interest charges

Interest: $24.78. We charge interest on unpaid tax and penalties from the due date of the return until the balance is paid in full. Interest stops growing if you pay the full amount due by November 2, 2026.

Removal or reduction of penalties

We understand that circumstances such as a serious illness, a natural disaster or a death in the family can make it hard to meet your tax obligations. If you believe you had a reasonable cause, call us at 800-829-4933 or write to us at the address on page 1.

Need help?
